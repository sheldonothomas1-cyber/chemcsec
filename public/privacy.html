ChemCSEC privacy policy

Applies to the ChemCSEC website, the Android app from Google Play and the Android APK. Last updated 14 November 2026.

In short: ChemCSEC has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to use the AI tutor, which sends your tutor messages to the AI company whose key you entered.

What is stored on your device

  • Your study progress: answers, mastery estimates, review schedule, mock exam results and checklists.
  • The first name and exam date you choose to enter (both optional).
  • AI tutor settings, including any AI key you paste in.

This data is kept in the app's local storage on your phone or browser. We (the developer) never receive it and have no server that stores it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it. You can also delete it any time with Settings → Delete all progress.

Backups

If you tap Save a backup, the app creates a file containing your progress and hands it to you. Where that file goes (your phone's files, Google Drive, a message to yourself) is your choice. AI keys are never included in backups.

The AI tutor (optional)

The tutor only works after you add your own key for Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude. When you send it a message, the app sends directly to that company:

  • the messages you type in the tutor conversation,
  • short notes from the app's own lessons that relate to your question,
  • sometimes the app's estimate of how well you know the topic.

Your name, exam date and the rest of your progress are not sent. That company handles the messages under its own terms: Google privacy policy and Gemini API terms; Anthropic privacy policy. Free Gemini keys may allow Google to use conversations to improve its products, so do not type personal information into the tutor.

Other connections

  • Lesson videos play from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) only when you open a lesson that has them. YouTube's privacy policy applies to the video player.
  • Links to other sites (CXC, textbooks, simulations) open only when you tap them.
  • The APK and website versions check GitHub at most once a day to see whether a newer version exists. This request contains no personal data. The Google Play version does not do this; Google Play handles its updates.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, no accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children and teenagers

ChemCSEC is made for secondary-school students preparing for CSEC Chemistry, usually aged 14 to 18. Because it collects no personal data, students can use it without an account. Students under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before setting up the optional AI tutor, since that involves a key from Google or Anthropic, whose own age rules apply.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/sheldonothomas1-cyber/chemcsec/issues, or use the contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
